# Educational quality evidence

Admissions currently emits audit events for implemented configuration and operational actions and stores lifecycle/outbox records. The broader academic quality-management foundation from Phase 03 remains the source of quality objectives, reviews, evidence, and corrective actions.

Admissions-specific controlled procedures, quality indicators, document retention, review sampling, and traceability from admission criteria to approved institutional policy are not complete. Existing records can support future evidence collection but do not establish an ISO or other certification. No compliance certification is claimed.
