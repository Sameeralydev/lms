# International policy mapping

The admission schema uses tenant locale/timezone fields, ISO-style currency storage, international country/region/address fields, and avoids globally requiring country-specific identity documents. Intake deadlines are represented as timestamps and parsed with intake timezone where applicable.

This is technical extensibility, not a jurisdictional legal review. No country-specific admissions, minors/guardian, fee, accessibility, retention, or data-residency policy is asserted. Institutions must supply approved rules and legal review before enabling public admissions. Regional policy evidence is deferred to authorized institution configuration and later compliance work.
